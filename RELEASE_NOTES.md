## What's new

- Your in-wallet AI assistant can now operate the wallet for you. Open the Agent tab and just say what you want — "what's my balance?", "send 5 EXFER to …", or "swap 0.001 BNB to EXFER". It does the legwork and always shows a confirmation you approve before any money moves; decline and nothing is sent.
- Mine EXFER right from your computer. The wallet now has a built-in CPU miner — start it from the assistant ("mine some EXFER"), it pays out to your address, and you can check its hashrate or stop it anytime.
- Faster, more reliable cross-chain swaps. The new flow locks the pool's side first, so you lock once and can walk away — a BNB→EXFER buy now settles in a few minutes instead of much longer, with honest step-by-step progress and safe auto-recovery if you close mid-swap.
